## Phase E: ball state after the goal (diagnosis; no rule built)

Demo frames 2690–2775 on the default variant (`all-gap05`) and on the baseline run, next to PFF and the verified labels.

**What vision sees after the shot:**
- **The real ball isn't detected from frame 2693 on,** in flight or in the net. The verified labels have it at pixel u ≈ 750 → 1380 in flight (2705–2720) and u ≈ 1530–1575 in the net (2730–2745).
- **Frame 2698: a false detection at the goal mouth** (pixel (1384, 603), conf 0.51, pitch (49.6, 2.5)). The real ball is near (700, 540) then. The gate reacquires on it (≥ 0.5, the track had been extrapolating for 4 frames). The 40 m/s rule zeroes its velocity, so the 0.5 s extrapolation holds it at (49.6, 2.5) to frame 2713.
- **Frames 2726–2764: more false detections,** at u ≈ 1100–1200 (pitch x 47–50, |y| < 2, inside the goal area), 350–400 px left of the labelled ball. They jitter at 3–10 m/s.
- **No vision sighting is past the goal line,** so `is_out` never fires. The state stays `alive` to 2764. After 2765 the camera leaves the pitch (grass share 0), the ball is null, and nothing is predicted.
- **Baseline ("max", 1 s extrapolation):** the extrapolation from the 2698 hit crossed x = 52.5 between the posts, so it went `dead` from 2699 (by luck). At 2739 the step from the held row to a new false detection read as a 5 m/s kick, so it went `alive` again to the end.

**The 21 rows over 2% after the goal event (90.1–92.1 s):**

| rows | video s | PFF `ball_state` | PFF p | vision p | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2703–2727 (9 rows) | 90.1–90.9 | alive (ball in flight, crosses the line at ~90.6 s) | 1.1–1.6% | 9–23% | the false 2698 ball held in front of goal (xG 0.43–0.69) |
| 2730–2763 (12 rows) | 91.0–92.1 | dead (in the net) | not scored | 3–15% | false detections in the goal area, `alive` |

- **The first 9 rows are alive in PFF too.** A stage 8 that matched PFF exactly would still leave them. They come from the false ball, which is Phase F's (hard negatives, missed balls).
- **For the other 12, vision has no real-ball cue** that the ball is in the net: the ball isn't detected there.

**A goal-mouth rule, measured on PFF before writing it.** Prototype: a visible ball past the goal line with |y| ≤ 3.66 m (and z < 2.44 when known) goes dead, and for `hold` seconds only a kick at the center spot restarts play. Run on all 64 PFF games through `vision.state.infer`:

| hold | dead → dead | alive → alive | alive → dead |
|---|---|---|---|
| off (today) | 6.69% | 81.02% | 0.58% |
| 10 s | 6.72% | 81.01% | 0.59% |
| 30 s | 6.72% | 80.95% | 0.65% |
| 90 s | 6.78% | 80.59% | 1.02% |

At 10 s it gains 1,219 dead frames and loses 725 alive ones across 64 games: a wash. Longer holds lose more alive frames than they gain dead ones. On the PFF dead frames within 60 s after a goal, today's rule gives null on 96.8% (no ball in the data), alive on 2.2% and dead on 1.0%. Nothing there for a rule to win.

**Ruling:** no Phase E code.
- The rule can't fire on the demo, because vision never sees the ball past the line, and it gains nothing measurable on PFF.
- The demo's done-when ("no row over 2% after the goal") can't be met by stage 8. Every one of those rows is a false detection with the real ball missed. That's Phase F.
- `vision.state_check` is unchanged (pooled: possession 77.9%, dead → dead or null 89.86%, alive → alive 81.03%).
- 03 stage 8 gets a known-limit note.
- Correction to Phase C's note: "the gate keeps the ball tracked in the net longer" was wrong. The gate kept a *false* ball tracked in front of the goal.

### What each option does downstream (read from the code)

- **Goal model features** (`prediction/features.py` `ball_track`, `ball_source = held`):
  - A ball row with x/y and `visible = True` is a fresh sighting, `interpolated` or not. 05 intends this: vision's extrapolation feeds the model.
  - Without a fresh row, the last one is held for `BALL_HOLD_S` (1 s), with `ball_age_s` growing; after that there's no ball.
  - Velocities come only from two fresh sightings 0.5 s apart.
  - PFF's aerial ball is ESTIMATED (`visible = False`), so the model was trained on exactly that: last ground ball held ≤ 1 s, then none. That's how PFF's own meter gets 2.2–2.6% on the demo's aerial rows.
  - `ball_z` is a model feature. Vision writes `z` null, so it's NaN on every vision row. PFF has z > 1 on only 78 of 64,911 VISIBLE ball rows (10517), so an airborne fresh ball with z is outside what the model saw.
- **Stage 8** (`vision/state.py`): reads every visible ball row. `carrier_max_z_m` (1 m) blocks a carrier only when `z` is set, so with null z an aerial ball's wrong ground position can hand the carrier to whoever is under it.
- **07 ball score** (`bench.ball_headline`): a hit needs a row that reaches game state (`has_xy`). A row whose position is withheld counts as a miss (`no_geometry`), even when its box is on the ball.
