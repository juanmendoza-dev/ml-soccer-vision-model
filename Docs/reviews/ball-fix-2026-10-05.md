### Rerunning without a vision run
Before this work `vision.replay` wrote replayed detections only, so a ball change couldn't reach stage 8 or inference without a full `vision.run` (~9 min), which would also overwrite the run. `vision.replay --variant TAG` now reruns stage 5 into `data/variants/TAG/` (cache and game state, the run's people kept), and `demo.check --rerun --variant TAG --set ...` runs it, stage 8 and inference there. Checked: the variant at the run's own config reproduces the demo's `objects.parquet` exactly (25,812 rows), and the demo check through it is identical to the table above.
