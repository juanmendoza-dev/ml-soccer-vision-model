### Rerunning without a vision run
Before this work `vision.replay` wrote replayed detections only, so a ball change couldn't reach stage 8 or inference without a full `vision.run` (~9 min), which would also overwrite the run. `vision.replay --variant TAG` now reruns stage 5 into `data/variants/TAG/` (cache and game state, the run's people kept), and `demo.check --rerun --variant TAG --set ...` runs it, stage 8 and inference there. Checked: the variant at the run's own config reproduces the demo's `objects.parquet` exactly (25,812 rows), and the demo check through it is identical to the table above.

## Phase B: label tool and verified labels (10-ball §1c; §4 step 2)

`scripts/ball_click.py --assist` (PFF ring, suggestion, auto-accept with a 10% spot check) and `--flag`. The user labeled all four clips on 2026-10-05.

| | label frames | ball `[x, y]` | `"none"` | auto-accepted kept | spot check | flags |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vb01 | 290 | 255 | 35 | 0 | turned off (bug, below) | 29, all reviewed |
| vb02 | 280 | 276 | 4 | — | — | 33 from the first pass, all reviewed |
| vb03 | 360 | 357 | 3 | 23 | 1 of 24 changed (4.2%): off | 49, all reviewed |
| demo01 | 281 | 243 | 38 | 39 | 0 of 4 changed: kept | 36, all reviewed |

- **Spot-check bug, found on vb01 and fixed (`16e5e14`):** a click 3+ px from the auto label counted as a change. The user's spot clicks were 1.3–5.7 px away, on the same ball, so auto-accept turned off and all 290 frames came back to the user: more clicking, same labels. A change is now another answer or more than 15 px (the hit radius). vb03's spot check turned off on a real change.
- **Flags:**
  - On vb03 and the demo, 61 flags (43 + 18) had the user's click within 10 px of a candidate at ≥ 0.5 while PFF's ball sat 30–185 px away. They come in stretches (vb03 7760–7985 and 8455–8535) where PFF's ball speed alternates between 0 and ~20 m/s on successive label frames: PFF lost the ball there.
  - At the user's choice these were marked reviewed without changing a label. The user looked at the other 24 and at every flag on vb01 and vb02.
