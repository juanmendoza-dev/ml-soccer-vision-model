## xG v1

- **CV, 5 folds by match:** LightGBM (7 leaves, 100 shots per leaf) beats the distance + angle logistic baseline on 5/5 folds, pooled log loss 0.298 vs 0.312, Brier 0.0862 vs 0.0901. **Chosen: LightGBM**, by 05's fixed rule. Best rounds 43–96. OOF sum of xG 507.6 for 508 goals; deciles within about 0.02 of the goal rate.
- **World Cup 2022 (StatsBomb 360, never trained on):** sum of xG 123.3 for 125 goals; log loss 0.292, Brier 0.0860. StatsBomb's own `statsbomb_xg` on the same shots: 107.2, 0.284, 0.0824, and it knows body part and technique, which xG v1 can't use (known only at the shot).
- **PFF game state (the same tournament, at the shot's grid row):** 1,154 open-play shots outside set-play phases, all with a held ball. Sum of xG 106.6 for 126 goals (15% low), log loss 0.328. The shortfall sits in the low bins (0.023 predicted vs 0.059 scored in the bottom fifth). PFF's held ball at the latest grid row at or before the shot isn't StatsBomb's strike location, and that's the difference this check was there to show. The 126 goals replace 05's "129 goals in 51 tracked games", which predates the full 64.

## P(goal) v1 on `lgbm-held-2026-09-27`

P(goal within H) = out-of-fold P(shot) × xG v1 at the same row. Every scored row has a held ball, so none is lost.

| H = 5 | PR-AUC vs goal | Brier | sum p |
|---|---|---|---|
| P(shot) × xG | **0.050** | 0.0029 | 2,431 |
| P(shot) alone | 0.044 | 0.0066 | 49,671 |
| xG alone | 0.016 | 0.0050 | 84,615 |

5,781 goal-positive rows (base rate 0.29%). H = 3: 0.056 vs 0.045 vs 0.021, 3,598 goal rows, sum of P(goal) 1,767.

- **Ranking:** the product ranks goals better than either part, at both horizons.
- **Calibration:** about 2.4× low at H = 5 (2,431 vs 5,781), 2.0× at H = 3. The top decile predicts 0.010 and sees 0.021.
- **Why: the v1 underestimate.** Median xG at the ball before a shot, as a share of xG at the shot's own row (rows where the shooter's team had the ball):

| before the shot | no goal | goal |
|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 0.67 | **0.27** |
| 2 s | 0.69 | 0.41 |
| 1 s | 0.84 | 0.53 |

Before goals the ball is far from where the shot comes from (median xG 0.035 at 5 s vs 0.108 at the shot), much more than before misses. On top of that, PFF's xG at the shot row itself is 15% low.

## What it means

- xG v1 is sound: calibrated out of fold, calibrated on World Cup 2022 360, close to StatsBomb's own model without the inputs that only exist at the strike.
- P(goal) v1 is a usable ranking but not a number to show as a probability yet: it's about half of the real goal rate.
- **Options for the overlay's headline number:**
  - **Recalibrate P(goal) v1 on PFF out-of-fold.** One monotone map fitted inside the CV training folds, so it stays out of fold. It's cheap and fixes the scale, but not the ranking.
  - **v2 (05):** xG at the likely shooter instead of the ball. That needs the parked "who will shoot" head.
  - **Interim:** xG at the shot location the shot model expects, for example the ball projected along its velocity. Still to be specced.
- Recalibration is the obvious next step, and it needs a short 05 addition first: the map, how it's fitted out of fold, and how it's checked.
