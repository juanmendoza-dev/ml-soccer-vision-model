# Evaluation: metrics and alarms

No defect was found in the headline PR-AUC calculation or its scored-row mask. Five issues affect lead-time diagnostics or alarm evaluation; only the rebound-bin issue directly changes a reported PR-AUC, and that is diagnostic AP rather than headline AP. Their prevalence and numerical impact in saved runs were not measured.

Reviewed `evaluation/metrics.py`, `evaluation/report.py`, `evaluation/runs.py`, `evaluation/compare.py`, and `scripts/lead_time.py`, after reading `CLAUDE.md` and specs 00, 02, 05, and 07. Supporting reads included label-event classification and the CV threshold-selection caller.

## Lead-time PR-AUC

- **Rebound positives disappear from the time-to-shot bins.** `scripts/lead_time.py:150` allows exact matches in the forward as-of join; `scripts/lead_time.py:259` then excludes positive rows with `tts=0` from every bin. With shots at 1 s and 3 s, the positive row at 1 s refers to the future rebound, but gets time-to-shot 0 instead of 2 s. Removing a poorly ranked rebound positive can inflate the relevant bin's AP and alter fold wins; the direction depends on the omitted predictions. **Severity: low. Confidence: CONFIRMED by an in-memory reproduction.** Relates to `Docs/Specs/05-prediction-model.md`, “Labels” and the strict `(t, t+H]` window, and `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “Metrics.” **Suggested fix:** use `allow_exact_matches=False` for the shot lookup and add a rebound case checking that every scored positive belongs to exactly one applicable time-to-shot bin. Headline AP is unaffected.

## Threshold selection and diagnostic optimism

- **The matched-budget diagnostic selects and evaluates τ on the same OOF outcomes.** `scripts/lead_time.py:128–137` sweeps pooled OOF predictions, selects the lowest observed miss rate within each false-alarm budget, and reports that selected result on the same shots. A threshold that happens to catch extra evaluation shots without extra false alarms is rewarded by both selection and evaluation, making the operating-point comparison optimistic. The output does say “descriptive,” but the module docstring's claim that τ is not picked on evaluation folds is incorrect. **Severity: med. Confidence: CONFIRMED by reading.** Relates to `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “Splits,” “Choosing τ,” and the descriptive-only report sweep. **Suggested fix:** choose a separate τ for each requested budget on each fold's inner validation matches and evaluate it on the outer fold; alternatively retain this as an explicitly labeled post-hoc best-on-OOF envelope, without treating it as an independently evaluated operating point. Main-report τ selection and headline AP are unaffected.

## Alarm coverage and false-alarm accounting

- **Missing masked predictions silently suppress alarms.** `evaluation/report.py:93–99` rejects stray prediction keys but accepts missing keys, filling them with null through a left join. Later checks require non-null p only on scored rows. A prediction writer that exports scored rows alone can therefore suppress high probabilities during masked set plays, despite the requirement to evaluate alarms over every row. An in-memory reproduction removed a masked high-p row: false alarms fell from 1 to 0 while AP remained 0.5. The shared join also exposes paired comparisons to this problem. **Severity: med. Confidence: CONFIRMED by execution.** Relates to `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “Alarms and lead time,” “Run format,” and “Report,” and `Docs/Specs/05-prediction-model.md`, “Inference.” **Suggested fix:** require explicit prediction-key coverage for all expected grid rows, distinguishing an absent row from an explicitly null prediction; also validate nullability against causal prediction eligibility, rather than the look-ahead label mask. No evidence was collected that current saved runs omit these rows. Headline AP is unaffected.

- **Gaps with no grid rows bypass the two-second alarm expiry.** `evaluation/metrics.py:144–152` checks staleness only when the current row has null p. High predictions at 0 s and 10 s, with no intervening rows, produce one alarm spanning the entire gap; a shot at 9 s receives 9 s lead, no miss, and no false alarm. **Severity: med. Confidence: CONFIRMED by an in-memory reproduction.** Relates to `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “Nulls hold, for a while,” and `Docs/Specs/05-prediction-model.md`, “Resampling” gaps. **Suggested fix:** expire an active alarm when elapsed time since its last prediction exceeds the cap, including across missing rows, before processing a fresh prediction; do not extend the expired interval to the eventual return row. Define its end timestamp consistently with the 10 Hz expiry rule. The spec states that current PFF periods have no internal grid gaps; that statement was not independently rechecked, so current PFF impact is unverified. Headline AP is unaffected.

- **Non-open-play goal-only events enter the shots table.** `evaluation/metrics.py:186–187` filters out `kind == "goal"`, but `prediction/labels.py:35` classifies non-open-play goals as `set_play` before distinguishing shots from goals. A corner own goal with no shot event therefore enters the shots table and can make an alarm true under a definition that requires a shot. A goal-only event was reproduced entering that table. Ordinary set-play goals with matching shot events merely duplicate coverage and do not change the reported alarm counts. **Severity: low. Confidence: CONFIRMED by execution.** Relates to `Docs/Specs/02-game-state-schema.md`, “Shots, goals and set plays” (goals without shots), and `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “True alarm.” **Suggested fix:** filter on the original `event_type == "shot"` when constructing the shots table, while retaining the open-play classification for those shots. If goal-only events should also validate warnings, explicitly change the evaluation definition instead. Occurrence in saved match data was not measured. Headline AP is unaffected.

## Checked and found clean

- **Ranking metrics:** average precision uses grouped ties and step integration, rather than trapezoidal PR area. ROC-AUC uses average ranks for ties; Brier is mean squared probability error. Existing tests include agreement with sklearn. This matches `Docs/Specs/07-evaluation.md`, “Metrics.”
- **Scored rows:** the mask is `label_mask_H` and not `all_estimated`, independent of p. Null scored predictions fail instead of being silently omitted. NaN and out-of-range probabilities are rejected when loading runs. This matches specs 05 and 07.
- **Pooling:** PFF and SkillCorner are reported separately; pooled AP is recomputed from concatenated OOF rows, not averaged across folds. Per-fold metrics and spread are also reported, as required by spec 07.
- **Paired comparison:** both runs must cover the same CV matches, their scored rows align against the same label tables, and wins require a strict majority of folds. IDSSE is excluded from model comparisons. This matches spec 07's paired-comparison rules.
- **Main τ selection:** the CV caller fits and selects on nested training/inner-validation matches, then applies the selected threshold to the held-out fold. Budget fallback, the budget-not-met flag, and higher-τ tie-breaking match spec 07. This is separate from the post-hoc diagnostic issue above.
- **Ordinary alarm behavior:** hysteresis, period boundaries, strictly-before-shot starts, grace matching, preference for the active alarm, shared starts for rebounds, and false-alarm denominators including quiet matches match spec 07. On an intact grid, long null stretches expire as specified.
- **Calibration:** quantile bins are used and tied predictions remain together; empty bins are omitted. This matches spec 07.
