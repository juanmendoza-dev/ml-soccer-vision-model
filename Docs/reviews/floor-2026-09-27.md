## Findings
- **It ranks well above chance, and every fold agrees.** H = 5: PR-AUC **0.167** against a 0.025 base rate (6.6×), ROC-AUC 0.918, per fold 0.167 ± 0.007. H = 3: PR-AUC 0.149 (base rate 0.016), ROC-AUC 0.947. This is the number the LightGBM baseline has to beat.
- **It's well calibrated** without any calibration step: the top decile predicts 0.166 and sees 0.170 positives.
- **It can't raise useful alarms, and that's expected for a floor.** Under the 3 false alarms per match budget, τ lands around 0.52 on every fold and **1,151 of 1,154 shots are missed**. The τ sweep shows why: catching 97% of shots (τ = 0.05) costs 108 false alarms per match, because the attacking team spends long spells near goal without shooting. Ball position says *where* a shot could come from, not *when*.
- **Budget transfer:** the τ chosen on inner matches gives 1.5–3.5 false alarms per match on the held-out folds (2.7 pooled), so the budget roughly holds out of sample.
- **Angle's coefficient is slightly negative given distance** (−0.11 standardized, vs −2.35 for distance). On its own angle is positive (+0.37); the two correlate at −0.53, so with distance in the model angle mostly separates wide-but-close positions from central ones. Not a bug; worth keeping in mind for the LightGBM features.
- **Newton steps diverged on the real data** (angle's tail is ~30σ at the goal line). Fixed with step halving; coefficients are stable to 3 decimals across folds.
- **Ball visibility:** 25.6% of scored rows have a ball that PFF marks as not visible (ESTIMATED). Whether PFF's estimated ball uses later frames is open (05); the floor uses it as-is.
- Runtime on the M1: about 1:45 for 64 games, both horizons, 5 folds + final.
